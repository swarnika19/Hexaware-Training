REPORT 1: PATIENT BILLING REPORT

This report provides a complete billing summary for each patient.

The report includes:
• Patient Name
• City
• Total Number of Appointments
• Total Bill Amount Generated
• Total Amount Paid
• Pending Amount

Purpose:
• To track patient billing and payment history.
• To identify patients with outstanding dues.
• To monitor hospital revenue collection.
• To analyze patient-wise financial contribution.
• To support hospital management in billing and payment decisions.

Key Insights:
• Patients with multiple appointments can be identified.
• Total revenue generated from each patient can be analyzed.
• Pending payment amounts can be tracked easily.
• Helps the finance department follow up on unpaid bills.
• Provides a consolidated view of patient billing activity.

Conclusion:
The Patient Billing Report gives a patient-wise summary of appointments, billing, payments, and outstanding balances, helping hospital management monitor financial performance and improve payment collection.s
